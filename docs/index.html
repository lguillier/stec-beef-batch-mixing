ANSES opinion 2016-SA-0121 · STEC in minced beef

How raw material boxes are filled and mixed into minced beef batches

Two interactive explainers for the raw material (VPH) and batch-mixing part of the model. Both are schematic illustrations of the model's logic, not data from real plants.

  1. Step 1 · contamination of VPH boxesFrom 60 carcasses to one VPH boxMeat pieces from 60 cattle fill a 1,000 kg box; only pieces from contaminated carcasses are explicitly simulated, following data/generate_vph_contamination.R.
  2. Step 2 · mixing into batchesRaw material units into eight batchesFatty, lean and frozen units are shared between successive 1,000 kg batches (60/30/10) with a 0.1% carry-over, after Figure 12 of the opinion.

Source code and data: github.com/lguillier/stec-beef-batch-mixing
